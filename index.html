I believe the best product decisions come from bringing the right people into the room early — developers, designers, SMEs, customers — and creating the conditions for honest, productive conversation. I invest time in building shared understanding before a line of code is written.

Stakeholder management is something I take seriously. I keep communication clear and consistent across all levels — from daily stand-ups with a Scrum team to progress presentations for directors.
